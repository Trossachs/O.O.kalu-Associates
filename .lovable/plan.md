# Rebuild the About Us 3D Book

## What will change
- Replace the current flat desktop spread and stacked mobile card with one responsive 3D book presentation.
- Give the closed book a clothbound/leather cover, brass title treatment, visible spine, page block, edge layering, and grounded shadows.
- Animate the front cover from closed to fully open when the section enters view, with a click/tap control to replay or toggle it.
- Shape the opened interior as a believable two-page spread with a center gutter, curved page shading, paper texture, and dimensional page edges.
- Keep the founder portrait framed on the left page and set the firm history and mission as editorial book copy on the right.
- Preserve reduced-motion accessibility by showing the book open without a flip animation.

## Responsive behavior
- Desktop uses a wide perspective with the full cover sweep and pronounced spine/page depth.
- Mobile keeps the same book geometry in a compact landscape format, with shorter editorial copy sized to remain readable and no flat-card fallback.

## Validation
- Verify the closed-to-open transition, interaction, reduced-motion behavior, and visual geometry at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Confirm the page has no browser errors and surrounding homepage sections remain unchanged.
